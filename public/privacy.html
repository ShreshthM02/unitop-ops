Privacy Policy

Unitop Ops App — Unitop Tours & Travel Pvt. Ltd. · Last updated: September 2026

Unitop Ops App is a private, internal application used exclusively by staff of Unitop Tours & Travel Pvt. Ltd. It is not available to the public and does not collect information from anyone outside the company's own staff. This page explains what data the application handles and how it is used.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the data of Unitop Tours & Travel staff members who are issued individual accounts to use Unitop Ops App, and the customer and vendor information those staff members enter into the system as part of running the company's tour operations.

What information is collected

  • Staff account information: name, role, and login credentials, used solely to identify who is using the system and control access to it.
  • Operational data entered by staff: tour queries, itineraries, cost sheets, quotations, vendor bookings, and payment records — information staff members type into the system as part of their normal work managing tours.
  • Documents uploaded by staff: files such as passport scans, signed contracts, and vendor confirmations, uploaded as part of a tour's paperwork.

How Google Drive access is used

Unitop Ops App uses the Google Drive API, under the restricted drive.file scope, solely to store documents that staff members explicitly upload through the application (for example, a scanned passport or a signed vendor contract attached to a tour file). This scope only grants access to files the application itself creates — it does not grant access to any other files in the connected Google account's Drive, and it is never used to read, search, or modify any pre-existing files or folders.

Uploaded files are stored in a Google Drive account owned and controlled by Unitop Tours & Travel Pvt. Ltd., organized into folders per tour file, solely for the company's own internal record-keeping.

How information is stored and secured

Operational data is stored in a managed, access-controlled database (Supabase/PostgreSQL). Access to the application requires an individually issued staff login. Data is used only for the internal operation of Unitop Tours & Travel's tour business.

What is not done with this data

This data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes, and is not used to train any third-party AI or machine learning model.

Data retention

Operational and document data is retained for as long as needed for the company's own record-keeping of its tour business, and is removed at the company's discretion when no longer needed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the data it describes can be sent to unitoptours@gmail.com.
